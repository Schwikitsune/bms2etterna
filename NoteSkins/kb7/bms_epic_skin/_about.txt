Notable features included in this noteskin

- Forced columnwidth to match 40 width in osu!mania

- Screen filter included in the noteskin itself to match the new columnwidth.
  Please disable the theme's screen filter in player options. Customize the included filter in "1 receptor.lua"

- Holds(long notes) are aligned so tails will end where the head starts

- There are no explosions by default, but explosions retain aspect ratio when scaling the notefield in customize gameplay.
  Should you add your own explosion textures, this means you won't get warped explosions when changing notefield scale
